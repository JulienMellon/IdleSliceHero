1. About this policy

This policy explains what information Slice Empire (the “Game”) collects, how it is used, who receives it, and the choices you have. By using the Game you agree to this policy.

2. Information we collect

We do not require an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.

a) Game data stored on your device

Your progress (cash, upgrades, story progress, settings, and a record of which purchases have been paid out) is saved on your iPhone. We do not receive it. Deleting the Game, or choosing Reset game in Settings, deletes it. There is no cloud save.

b) Analytics and crash reports (collected automatically)

We use PostHog to count how the Game is used and Sentry to find and fix crashes.

  • Install ID. The first time the Game runs it makes a random number on your iPhone. It is the only identity analytics has. It is not your name, your Apple ID or your advertising identifier, and it is not shared with the advertising partners in section 2(c). You can see it in Settings, on the Install ID card.
  • What PostHog receives: the Install ID, when a session starts and ends, and in-game events that carry only ids and counts (for example that an ad was offered, started or finished, that the shop screen was viewed, that a purchase completed, that a story scene was seen, that a pizzeria was built), together with the device model, iOS version, app version, language and screen size that its software adds to each event. We turn off its lookup of a location from your IP address, so we do not get a country from it. Like any internet request, yours reaches the provider from your IP address.
  • What Sentry receives when the Game crashes or reports an error: the error and where it happened in the code, the device model, iOS version and app version. We do not send names, emails or other personal details with it, and crash reports are not tied to the Install ID.
  • Your choice. Switch off Share anonymous analytics in Settings and nothing more is sent to PostHog or Sentry, and anything still waiting to be sent is dropped. The choice is kept on your iPhone.

c) Advertising

The Game shows rewarded video ads, which you choose to watch in exchange for in-game rewards. Ads are served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect and use: your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking, other device and app identifiers, your IP address and the coarse location it gives, device and iOS information, the ads you view and how you interact with them (for example video views), and performance data about the ads SDK (launch time, hangs and energy use). If you decline tracking, ads are still shown but are not personalised using your IDFA. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form is shown before the first ad where the law requires it; if you refuse, no ad is shown and the Game carries on.

Switching off analytics in Settings does not change advertising: use the iOS tracking choice in section 5.

d) Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details. We learn what was bought through RevenueCat, which receives purchase receipts and a random anonymous app user ID so that Restore Purchases works across your devices. The Game keeps a local record of which purchases have been paid out so that none is paid twice.

e) Communications

If you email us, we receive your email address and your message so we can reply.

3. How we use information

  • To run, maintain and improve the Game (including fixing crashes and balancing gameplay).
  • To show ads and to measure whether ads work.
  • To process purchases and restore them.
  • To respond to support requests.
  • To comply with law and protect against fraud or abuse.

We do not sell your personal information for money. Section 6 explains how advertising may count as “sharing” or “selling” under some state laws.

5. Tracking choices (iOS)

The Game uses your advertising identifier for tracking only if you allow it in the iOS tracking prompt. You can change your choice at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, and reset your advertising identifier in iOS settings. Declining tracking does not remove ads; they just are not personalised using your IDFA.

6. Your rights

All users: you can ask us what data we hold about you, ask us to delete it, or opt out of analytics. Because we do not know who you are, we ask for your Install ID (Settings, Install ID card) to find your usage data. The support page shows how. Email SUPPORT_EMAIL.

EEA, UK, Switzerland (GDPR): our legal bases are legitimate interests (running and securing the Game, crash fixing), consent (personalised advertising, where required) and contract (purchases). You may access, correct, delete, restrict or port your data, object to processing, withdraw consent at any time, and complain to your local data protection authority. Data may be transferred outside your country, including to the United States, using safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

California and other US states (CCPA/CPRA and similar): you may request to know, delete or correct personal information, and opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising. Sharing advertising identifiers with ad partners may count as such. You can opt out by declining the iOS tracking prompt or by emailing us. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.
