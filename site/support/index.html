Common questions

My progress is gone.

The game saves on your iPhone only. Deleting the app, or choosing Reset game in Settings, deletes the save. There is no account and no cloud save yet, so we cannot restore it for you.

I bought something and did not get it.

Open Settings in the game and tap Restore Purchases. If it still is not there, email us the date of the purchase and the order ID from Apple's receipt. Apple handles refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com.

The game does not play sound.

The game follows your ring/silent switch. Turn it to ring, and check Music and Sound in the game's Settings.

Ask for your data, or to delete it

You can ask what data we hold about you, ask us to delete it, or turn off analytics yourself. You do not need an account: the game has none.

  1. Open the game, go to Settings, and find the Install ID card and tap Copy.
  2. Email SUPPORT_EMAIL with the subject Data request, say whether you want a copy of your data or want it deleted, and paste the Install ID.
  3. We find the usage data tied to that ID and answer within 30 days.

The Install ID is a random number made on your iPhone. It is not linked to your name. Crash reports are not tied to it, so we cannot look one up by ID; they contain no name or email.

To stop analytics and crash reports at any time, switch off Share anonymous analytics in Settings. Ads and purchases are handled by Google and Apple, see the privacy policy.
