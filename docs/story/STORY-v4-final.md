## The B-plot: Todd

**Logline.** The MegaSlice rep who loses every neighbourhood is one man, Todd Smith, a salesman who physically cannot lie. Each loss gets him demoted. Since minute one he has also been the shop's most loyal customer, "Mr. Smith," in a trench coat with a mustache drawn on in marker. Smith is his real name, and a drawn-on mustache isn't a lie. Because he can't lie, he keeps blurting out MegaSlice's next move. In the finale he quits on the front page, Victor finally says his name right, Nonna hires him, and he names the villain waiting in Boston: Trish Vance.

**Running gags**

- Victor never gets his name right: Tad → Rod → Tom → Dodd → Ted → Toddler → "...Todd." ("Took you seven tries.")
- Name-tag demotions: VP of Expansion → Senior Associate → Associate → Intern → Unpaid Intern → Vending Restocker → Slicey (the mascot) → TODD · ROSA'S.
- Dramatic irony: the player knows Mr. Smith is Todd from the first panel. The cast works it out slowly; Priya proves it with data, and Genevieve knew all along.

**How it weaves in.** It's a background gag through Waterfront. In the long gap, three Mr. Smith scenes each leak a bit more of the University scheme. Priya's investigation becomes part of her decision whether to leave. Todd is the "mysterious" person who removed the vending machines. He delivers Victor's yacht bribe, confesses to Genevieve, and joins Rosa's in the finale.

## What the build needs (later)

- **Interlude scenes.** A new interlude kind with the heading "Suburbs · Interlude". Interludes obey the 90 s gap, and main beats win ties.
- **Scene order is guaranteed.** When a later scene depends on an interlude, it waits for that interlude; nothing is dropped. Interludes that block a later scene trigger on play time since their prerequisite. The others trigger on delivery counts set from the sim. Waterfront's "Man Overboard" plays the moment the bid opens, and the bid needs it seen first.
- **New dependency.** Uptown's Act 3 waits for Industrial's finale, because Todd's mascot demotion follows that loss. While it waits, the goal bar shows "Win over Industrial to continue Uptown's story". Check this in the pacing sim.
- **Art.** Todd reuses the MegaSlice rep. New: a trench-coat-and-mustache variant (plus one without the mustache), a Slicey foam costume, and props `order-slip`, `name-tag` and `phone-footage`.
- **Cuts.** Victor's "This war is not over, pizza person" is cut for room in the finale.
